# ThirdAbove 🎵 — User Guide

> *Master the art of vocal duet. Learn to lock in harmonies and sing with confidence.*

Welcome to **ThirdAbove**, a specialized vocal trainer built to train your ear and voice to hold beautiful two-part harmonies alongside a lead singer without getting pulled off pitch.

---

## 🎧 Best Setup: Headphones Recommended
For the ultimate training experience, **wear wired or wireless stereo headphones**:
- **Left Ear (🎧 Left Channel):** The **Lead Note** (Root Melody).
- **Right Ear (🎧 Right Channel):** Your **Harmony Target**.
- **Spatial Separation:** Panning the two notes into separate ears prevents acoustic masking in your head, making it far easier to isolate the two distinct notes while your brain learns how the chord resonates.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Launch & Splash Screen
- Open **ThirdAbove** on your Android phone.
- Enjoy the warm, inspiring splash screen (*"Tuning your vocal space..."*) and tap **"Start Singing"**.

### 2. Choose Your Vocal Range
At the top of the trainer, select your vocal category:
- **Tenor (Default):** Roots start at **E3 to A3**, putting your 3rd harmonies comfortably in the **G#3 to C#4** sweet spot (no straining for high A4s!).
- **Baritone:** Roots start at **C3 to F3** (harmonies in E3 to A3).
- **Bass:** Roots start at **G2 to C3** (harmonies in B2 to E3).
- **Alto / Countertenor:** Roots start at **A3 to D4** (harmonies in C#4 to F#4).
- **Soprano / High Lead:** Roots start at **C4 to G4** (harmonies in E4 to B4).

### 3. Select a Harmony Interval
Choose the harmonic interval you want to master:
- **Major 3rd Above (+4 semitones):** *(Recommended for beginners)* The classic, sweet, uplifting pop and folk harmony.
- **Minor 3rd Above (+3 semitones):** Moody, bluesy, and soulful harmony.
- **Perfect 4th Above (+5 semitones):** Modern, open suspension interval.
- **Perfect 5th Above (+7 semitones):** Powerful rock and hymn anchor note.
- **Major 6th Above (+9 semitones):** Rich country, R&B, and gospel interval.
- **Unison (0 semitones):** Pure unison ear alignment test.

### 4. Audition the Notes
- Tap **"Play Left (4s)"** to hear the lead note alone in your left ear.
- Tap **"Play Right (4s)"** to hear your target harmony alone in your right ear.
- Tap **"🎧 Play Stereo Duet (L: Lead | R: Harmony)"** to hear both notes ring simultaneously in full stereo harmony!

### 5. Start the Microphone
- Tap the **Microphone button** in the top right to begin live tracking.
- *(Note: While any reference tone is sounding, the app automatically mutes the microphone to prevent self-feedback from the speaker!)*

## 🎯 The Drill & Scoring System

### The Real-Time Pitch Gauge
- **Sing your harmony note:** Watch the gauge needle respond in real-time.
- **Locked In Harmony (Green):** Within $\pm 25\text{ cents}$ of perfect pitch.
- **Slightly Sharp / Flat (Amber):** Within $\pm 45\text{ cents}$.
- **Too High / Low (Coral):** Further than $45\text{ cents}$ off target.
- **Anti-Lead Alert (Coral):** If you succumb to the instinct of singing the melody note instead of the harmony note, the app alerts you: *"Pulled to lead! Sing the harmony higher!"*

### 2-Second Pitch Lock-In Progression
- When your pitch enters the green in-tune zone ($\pm 25\text{ cents}$), the **Pitch Lock-in bar** begins filling.
- **Hold steady for 2.0 seconds:** The app awards mastery (*"🎉 Mastered! Moving to next note..."*) and **automatically advances to the next lead note** in your sequence, playing the new note so you can continue your practice flow.

### Smart 2-Second Re-orientation Fail-Safe
- If you start singing but struggle out of tune for **2 consecutive seconds**, ThirdAbove will chime in and **play both notes simultaneously for 2 seconds in stereo** (*"🎧 Reorienting: Listen to the harmony duet..."*), immediately re-grounding your musical memory!

## 💡 Practical Duet Tips
1. **The 70/30 Listening Rule:** When harmonizing with your wife, listen to her voice at roughly 70% attention and your own voice at 30%. You should feel your voice *lock into* hers rather than competing with it.
2. **Feel the Resonance:** When you hit a true Major 3rd, you will feel a pleasing physical "buzz" or warmth between the two voices. 
3. **Practice Softly First:** Singing softly makes it easier to hear both pitches without shouting yourself out of tune.
