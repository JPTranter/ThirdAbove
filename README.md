# ThirdAbove 🎵

> *Find your harmony. Master the art of vocal duet.*

**ThirdAbove** is a native Android vocal trainer designed to help singers learn and practice singing vocal harmonies (3rds, 6ths, 4ths, 5ths, and dynamic counter-melodies) alongside a lead voice.

## Features
- **Real-Time Pitch Detection (YIN Algorithm):** High-precision low-latency pitch tracking with cents deviation.
- **Harmonization Training Drills:** Step-by-step interval ear-and-voice exercises designed to break the instinct of drifting into the lead melody.
- **Visual Pitch Ribbon:** Real-time feedback visualizer showing reference lead notes and your vocal performance.
- **Dual-Track Duet Player:** Practice full songs with independent lead and harmony volume sliders.
